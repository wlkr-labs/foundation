## Keep the accounting small and accurate

Copy the empty [support ledger](../templates/support-ledger.csv) into `private/support-ledger.csv`. Record gross receipts, fees, net receipts, refunds, purpose, and private evidence references. Record expenditures separately using the same ledger's entry type and category columns.

Use entry types `support`, `expense`, `refund`, and `allocation`. Cash receipts are positive; expenses and refunds are negative. For support, net equals gross minus processor fees; do not record those same withheld fees again as an expense. Allocation rows have zero cash movement and use `allocation_usd` to show amounts reserved or released. Useful categories are software, education, formation, founder compensation, and taxes.

Keep formation allocations and tax reserves distinct from spendable funds. A transfer into a reserve is an allocation, not an expense; do not double-count it when it is later spent. Reconcile the ledger to actual processor and bank statements. Export statements and retain receipts privately.

Crowdfunding receipts may be taxable depending on the facts. Calling them donations does not resolve that question; receiving a tax form is not the sole test. Determine treatment from the actual arrangement and keep good records before using the full balance. [IRS crowdfunding guidance](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/some-things-to-know-about-crowdfunding-and-taxes).

## Spending and founder compensation

Proposed order of decisions:

1. Account for fees, refunds, tax obligations, and already-committed costs.
2. Maintain the public tools and pay necessary mission expenses.
3. Allocate an affordable amount to formation and operating reserves.
4. Pay modest, documented compensation for actual development, teaching, and maintenance when revenue supports it.

Most available resources should serve creation, maintenance, access, and education. Development pay can itself be mission spending, but report founder compensation separately so supporters can understand the arrangement. Do not advertise a numerical split until a workable budget has been adopted.

Before incorporation, describe the actual personal operating arrangement. After formation, have disinterested directors approve reasonable pay using comparable work, a defined scope, affordability, and recorded decisions. The founder should not approve their own pay. [IRS private-benefit and compensation guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/stay-exempt/maintaining-501c3-tax-exempt-status-course-video-transcript).
