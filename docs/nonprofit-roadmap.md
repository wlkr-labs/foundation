# Nonprofit roadmap

Status: planning draft, October 6, 2026. The current initiative is unincorporated and has no IRS determination. The practical route is a Texas nonprofit corporation, followed by an application appropriate to the actual activities and finances.

## Stage 1 — Free preparation now

- Maintain the six plans in this folder and identify the actual people the work benefits.
- Build evidence of religious education and technical instruction through useful public outputs.
- Prepare accurate website status/support copy and select one funding method when ready.
- Keep financial records and a separate formation allocation.
- Identify two potential directors who will provide real oversight alongside Shane.
- Inventory code, domains, licenses, contracts, and accounts before proposing asset transfers.

Complete this stage when the mission, initial programs, financial-record method, and next practical tasks are documented. It does not require a subscription or a filing.

## Stage 2 — Corporation when funding and people are ready

Texas nonprofit formation has a $25 base fee. The corporation needs at least three directors, a president and a different secretary, and a registered agent/qualifying Texas office. A resident individual can serve as agent; the public street address must permit service during normal business hours. [Texas formation instructions](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/instructions/202.shtml), [Texas nonprofit requirements](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/nonprofitfaqs.shtml).

Prepare the following packet using confirmed names, addresses, and activities:

- Name-availability review and certificate of formation.
- Specific 501(c)(3) purpose, limits on private benefit and political activity, and dedication of dissolution assets to eligible public purposes.
- Bylaws, conflict policy, initial resolutions, banking authority, budget, and records policy.
- Written ownership/permission arrangements for the domain, software, and other transferred assets; preserve third-party and Scripture-text rights.

Texas's minimum form does not supply every IRS provision. Include appropriate organizing clauses from the start. [IRS sample organizing documents](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/sample-organizing-documents-public-charity).

File after the actual directors agree and the packet is approved. Obtain the EIN directly from the IRS for free, then establish finances in the entity's name. [IRS EIN service](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/employer-identification-number).

## Governance outline to develop before adoption

Proposed model: a board-governed corporation without voting members. It keeps governance simple while allowing supporters and contributors.

| Document | Decisions to put into the future draft |
| --- | --- |
| Bylaws | Purpose; three or more directors; selection and terms; quorum; remote meetings; permitted written consent; officers; conflicts; removal and succession |
| Conflict policy | Annual disclosures; related-party transactions; founder recusal; disinterested approval and minutes |
| Initial resolutions | Adopt documents; appoint officers; authorize EIN, banking and exemption application; adopt budget and recordkeeping |
| Compensation resolution | Actual duties, time, comparables, affordable amount, disinterested approval and review date |
| Asset resolution | Exact assets and rights; transfer or license terms; contracts and third-party permissions |

These outlines create no board, employment agreement, salary, or binding corporate action. Draft final language from confirmed facts; record genuine votes and signatures.

## Stage 3 — Federal recognition

The $300 base budget combines Texas's $25 fee and the IRS's $275 Form 1023-EZ fee. The full Form 1023 costs $600, making the combined base $625 if that application is required. Add any transaction fees or other chosen expenses. [IRS fees](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-and-1023-ez-amount-of-user-fee).

Before selecting EZ, complete every eligibility-workbook question. Key financial tests concern annual gross receipts no greater than $50,000 in the relevant past/projected years and assets no greater than $250,000. Review prior commercial operations and succession/asset-transfer facts; lack of a registered business does not by itself settle every eligibility question. [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez).

Request public-charity classification based on a genuine plan for public support. Keep records of funding sources and monitor the applicable support test as the organization matures. Broad public access alone is not the financial public-support test. [IRS public-charity classification](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/eo-operational-requirements-private-foundations-and-public-charities).

An authorized officer signs the appropriate application. Save the submitted form, payment receipt, organizing documents, and eventual determination. Preparation and application submission are separate from approval.

## Stage 4 — State exemptions and public transition

After IRS recognition, apply for applicable Texas sales/franchise exemptions through AP-204 with the determination letter. Track state reporting obligations until the Comptroller grants the exemption; incorporation alone does not grant it. [Texas application process](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/exempt/501%28c%29-exemptions-1.php), [reporting until exemption](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/faq/exemptions.php).

Update website and payment accounts to identify the actual entity, effective status, and recipient. Reconcile pre-formation support and transfers rather than rewriting historical personal payments as charitable donations.

Texas generally does not require most charities to register for charitable solicitation, but assess other states' requirements before geographically targeted fundraising or expansion. [Texas Attorney General](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/divisions/charitable-trusts/registration-and-filings).

## Stage 5 — Keep it useful and current

- File the applicable IRS annual return. Eligible small organizations generally use free 990-N; calendar-year filings are normally due May 15. Three consecutive missed required filings cause automatic revocation. [IRS filing guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/annual-electronic-notice-form-990-n-for-small-organizations-faqs-when-to-file), [free filing](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/annual-electronic-notice-form-990-n-for-small-organizations-faqs-how-to-file).
- Respond to the Texas periodic report when requested, no more often than once every four years; the timely filing fee is $5. [Texas periodic report](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/instructions/802.shtml).
- Have the board approve the annual financial report and document decisions, conflicts, compensation, and public-support review.
- Continue the small maintenance/education rhythm and publish accurate project status and funding use.

Formation is complete when the accepted certificate, EIN, IRS determination, applicable state exemptions, actual board records, and entity finances are in place. Continuing success is useful public work and sustained stewardship.
